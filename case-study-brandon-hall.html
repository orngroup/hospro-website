Case study · Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa

One platform, from first enquiry to signed agreement.

Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa is a country house hotel in 17 acres of Warwickshire gardens and woodland, with 14 spaces for meetings, weddings and celebrations. HOSPRO now runs its events sales, venue data, maintenance planning and bar stock in one place.

The challenge

Plenty of demand, too many places to keep track of it.

Enquiries for meetings, weddings and celebrations reached the hotel from every direction: phone, email, the website and agents such as arrangeMY and Hitched. Each one needed details taking, a quote writing, follow-up and, eventually, a contract.

The information the team needed to do that well, from room capacities and technology to packages, rates and supplier requirements, lived in different documents, inboxes and people's heads. Behind the scenes, equipment and room condition weren't recorded in one place, and bar stock relied on periodic counts.

The approach

Build the hotel into HOSPRO first.

We started with the venue itself: every function room measured, its capacities recorded for every layout and its technology audited. That single set of facts now drives the sales tools, the proposals the client receives and the hotel's own website.

We then mapped the events team's real day, from the first enquiry through quote, acceptance, agreement and deposits to confirmation, and built HOSPRO around it, with practical tools for the bar and maintenance teams alongside.

What we built

HOSPRO at Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa.

SalesPRO

Enquiries and pipeline

Every enquiry lands in one dashboard with the full brief, and moves through the pipeline to confirmation.

  • Enquiries from the hotel website's event planner go straight in, complete with room, layout and requirements
  • Events Concierge chat, a friendly guided conversation that turns a website visitor into a complete brief
  • Budget, accommodation needs and source channel captured on every enquiry
  • Deposit, second deposit, instalment and balance tracking
SalesPRO

Quotes and proposals

Quotes built in minutes from the hotel's own packages, and sent as proposals that look like the hotel.

  • Day delegate and 24-hour packages, accommodation blocks on bed and breakfast or dinner, bed and breakfast, à la carte catering and custom lines
  • A and B options when a client wants a choice
  • Branded proposal with a to-scale seating plan and an event carbon estimate
  • Online acceptance with option selection, an automatic two-week hold, and online agreement signing
EventsPRO

Venue data and seating plans

One accurate record of every space, used everywhere the hotel sells.

  • 17 function rooms with measurements, capacities for theatre, cabaret, boardroom, U-shape and reception, and in-room technology
  • Seating plans drawn to each room's real dimensions
  • Supplier fact sheets with insurance and PAT compliance checks
  • A beverage management dashboard with stock value, weekly usage and CSV export
StayCORP

Groups and guests

The tools that make residential events and weddings easier to run.

  • Group Room Configurator: enter adults, children and babies and HOSPRO allocates them across room types, with extra beds, sofa beds and cots
  • Printable, branded allocation sheet
  • Guest pre check-in link, building the hotel's own guest database
  • Post-stay feedback page
AssetPRO

Maintenance and investment

A clear, costed picture of what each room needs before money is spent.

  • Equipment and condition audit of every function room
  • Costed upgrade plan with editable prices and quantities
  • Budget approval sheet for the COO and per-room job sheets for the maintenance team
  • Excel export with purchase links for ordering
iPad app

Bar stock on the floor

A simple iPad app for the bar team, set up from the hotel's stock take.

  • PIN login for staff
  • 117 products loaded, with low and out-of-stock filters
  • Record usage and deliveries as they happen
  • Re-order in one tap, emailed to the general manager
Seating plans, drawn to scale

Clients see their event before they book it.

Every function room at Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa is held in HOSPRO with its real measurements, so seating plans are drawn to scale for the layout and guest numbers the client asks for. This one shows the Woodlands Suite set cabaret style for 160 guests.

The same plans appear in HOSPRO proposals and in the event planner on the hotel's website, so what the client sees online, what the events team quotes and what operations set up all match.

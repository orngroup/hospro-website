01 · Finance & procurement

HOSPRO Finance Office

A dedicated back-office finance team that gives owners and general managers a clear, timely view of performance — and takes the transactional workload off the property.

  • Monthly management accounts and owner reporting
  • Annual budgets and full-year rolling forecasts
  • Year-end packs prepared for your statutory accountants
  • VAT management and bank reconciliation
  • Accounts payable and receivable
  • Payroll administration
  • Group purchasing — preferential supplier pricing for HOSPRO hotels
On the platform: spend, supplier and margin data flows into HOSPRO's procurement and profit dashboards, so savings are tracked month by month.
02 · Hotel operations

Operational Excellence

Great hotels are run well every single day. We work alongside your team to set the standards, remove friction and make every department perform — whether you are established, repositioning or opening your doors for the first time.

  • Department-by-department performance reviews
  • Standard operating procedures and service standards
  • Pre-opening, launch and repositioning support
  • Maintenance, compliance and asset upkeep
  • Guest satisfaction tracking and service recovery
  • Cost control and labour efficiency
On the platform: tasks, maintenance and room status sit in HOSPRO's operations module — so issues are logged, owned and closed, not forgotten.
03 · Yield management

Revenue & Yield Intelligence

Our revenue strategists optimise every rate, channel and date — balancing occupancy and average rate to grow RevPAR and profit, not just top-line revenue.

  • Dynamic pricing and rate strategy
  • Demand forecasting and pick-up analysis
  • Channel mix and distribution cost control
  • Direct booking growth and OTA dependency reduction
  • Corporate and group rate negotiation
  • Weekly revenue meetings with clear actions
On the platform: HOSPRO's AI highlights pricing opportunities and StayCORP manages corporate accounts and negotiated rates in one place.
04 · Sales & marketing

Commercial Growth

Sales, events and marketing working as one commercial engine. Data-led, guest-focused campaigns for every kind of property — from luxury country estates to boutique city hotels.

  • Commercial strategy and annual sales plan
  • Account management and pipeline development
  • Meetings, events and weddings sales
  • Targeted digital and email campaigns
  • Market and competitor insight
  • Brochures, proposals and sales collateral
On the platform: every enquiry, proposal and booking lives in HOSPRO's sales pipeline and events tools — with conversion reported live.

On the platform: HOSPRO's content and guest experience tools keep messaging consistent and feedback visible to every team.
